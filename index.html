Install on iOS Safari

To install FDE Mastery as an offline native app on your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Tap the Share icon in the bottom Safari toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

The app will launch in full standalone mode and remain functional with zero connectivity.
